PadhaiKaro helps UPSC and SSC students organise study plans, notes, practice, and progress. We
collect only the data required to provide those features: account email, password hash, study-plan
data, study activity, user-created notes/uploads, optional voice notes, optional notification token,
and optional connected-calendar token. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

We use data to authenticate users, sync their study workspace, generate requested study features,
deliver configured reminders, and maintain service security. We do not sell personal data or use
precise location. AI providers receive only the note/audio content a user explicitly submits for an
AI action, subject to the provider configuration disclosed at launch.

Data travels over HTTPS in production. Session credentials use device secure storage where
available. Users may delete their account and associated server data from the app or request help at
the support contact. Backups may retain deleted data until their documented expiry period.

We may use a crash-reporting or analytics provider only after it is configured to receive a
minimised, pseudonymous event set. It must not receive raw notes, audio, answers, passwords,
authentication tokens, or email addresses.

This draft must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel and completed with real retention periods,
provider names, support details, jurisdiction, and a public HTTPS URL before release.
